# Shopping List

Parts for [Plan A and Plan B](README.md#power). The prices come from listings
that a web search found on 2026-11-06. Prices change often. "est." marks an
estimate that no listing supports.

## Before you buy

- **Already on hand:** the Rockpals 250 W power station (240 Wh, pure sine).
- **Bench stock to use first** (see [inventory.md](../../inventory.md)): WAGO
  221-415 connectors (about 21 spare), the multimeter, the strippers, the
  heat-shrink kit and the ferrule crimper. This project takes nothing from stock
  yet.
- **Buy used:** the stands, the beach cart and surplus commercial floods. Test
  each used item before a session.
- **Buy factory-refurbished only:** batteries and power stations. A used lithium
  battery has unknown health unless you do a capacity test.
- **Buy new:** cords and fuses. Damaged insulation on wet sand is not worth the
  risk.

**Budget flood with no wiring:** LEPOWER 50 W, sold as a 2-pack. It gives about
4,500–5,000 lm, is IP66, and has a cord with a plug and an inline switch. It
gives about a third less light than the Halco. The plug and the switch are not
waterproof.
